## Key Decisions Made

### 1. Prime Directive Customization
**Decision**: Adapted the prime directive specifically for HERMES_Quantum's focus on quantum computing stock analysis with multi-agent architecture.

**Rationale**: The directive must reflect the project's specific goals and success criteria. HERMES_Quantum requires complete understanding of all agents, data sources, and analytical capabilities.

### 2. Anti-Sampling Directive Emphasis
**Decision**: Made the anti-sampling directive a critical, prominent feature of master_protocol.md.

**Rationale**: Sampling or partial reading leads to missed dependencies and incomplete understanding. 100% fidelity is essential for achieving the prime directive.

### 3. Special Characters Ban
**Decision**: Strictly forbid special characters and emojis in generation_sub_protocol.md.

**Rationale**: Maintains professional code standards, prevents encoding issues, and ensures compatibility across systems.

### 4. Comprehensive Logging System
**Decision**: Implemented detailed logging with specific log types and templates.

**Rationale**: Prevents context loss, enables restart capability, provides audit trail, and ensures accountability.

### 5. Tool/Reference Request Process
**Decision**: Created separate sub-protocols for identification and request of tools/references.

**Rationale**: Separates evaluation from acquisition, ensures thorough research, and prevents unnecessary resource additions.

### 6. Restart Protocol Emphasis
**Decision**: Created detailed restart protocol with comprehensive preparation steps.

**Rationale**: Context window limitations are inevitable in long projects. Proper restart preparation prevents lost work and maintains continuity.
